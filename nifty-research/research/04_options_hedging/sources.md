# Sources read on 1 October 2026

- [OCC Options Industry Council strategy guide](https://www.optionseducation.org/getmedia/68305977-b772-41c8-bf1d-3d405725b3cf/options-strategies-quick-guide-2025.pdf). Used for the leg definitions and terminal payoff interpretation. Its US equity assignment details do not transfer to Nifty's European cash-settled contracts.
- [OIC short straddle](https://www.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/short-straddle). Source for the short-premium structure and its unbounded upside exposure. The replay does not assume a stop caps that loss.
- [Derman and Kamal, When you cannot hedge continuously](https://emanuelderman.com/wp-content/uploads/1998/12/risk-non_continuous_hedge.pdf). Original discrete-hedging analysis. Its constant-volatility setting informs the separately labelled simulation and the frequency-versus-replication-error question.
- [QuantLib discrete hedging example](https://github.com/lballabio/QuantLib/blob/master/Examples/DiscreteHedging/DiscreteHedging.cpp). Primary maintained implementation inspected for the self-financing delta-hedge cash account, final payoff and hedge unwind. This study independently implements a small Python calculation. It does not install, run or claim to validate QuantLib.
- [Buehler, Gonon, Teichmann and Wood, Deep Hedging](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.03042). Original paper accounts for trading frictions and risk-sensitive objectives. It motivates treating hedging as a constrained cost/risk problem, not treating continuous Black-Scholes delta as an executable policy.
- [Hans Buehler's Deep Hedging repository](https://github.com/hansbuehler/deephedging). Primary codebase inspected for its distinction between decision-time features, future hedge outcomes and monetary utility. No neural hedger is trained in this study. Its synthetic results are not imported as evidence about Nifty.
- [Dhan funds and margin API](https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/funds/). Documents margin calculation, including a multi-order endpoint. No account API is called. Historical available funds, SPAN, exposure margin, offsets and temporary legging margin remain unavailable.

Fees come from the shared `nifty_lab.config.ResearchConfig`, whose source validation belongs to the central research lab. The saved `artifacts/results.json` includes the exact values used. These are estimates for separate executed orders and exclude settlement or exercise because this experiment closes intraday.
