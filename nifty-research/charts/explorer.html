Charts that need more data
  • Exact footprint, bid/ask trade delta and exact volume profile need a complete trade tape with aggressor classification.
  • Spot/futures basis and constituent breadth need synchronised Nifty spot and weighted constituent prices.
  • IV smile, term structure, Greeks and VIX comparisons need reliable IV inputs, spot, rates, expiry timing and wider expiry coverage.
  • Dealer gamma exposure needs positioning assumptions. OI alone cannot identify who holds each side.
  • News overlays need time-stamped historical news.
