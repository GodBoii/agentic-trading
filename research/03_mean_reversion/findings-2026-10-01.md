## Incomplete exposure and data limits

Rolling fade ended August 19 with an unresolved 51-share short in security2955. Its last-quote liquidation estimate was INR-28.6572, separate from closed P&L. The recorded mark was 0.982107 seconds before tape end, but no later executable exit arrived. Do not call the INR-9,246.31 closed subtotal a fully liquidated account result. Adding that unverified marked estimate would produce approximately INR-9,274.97, not a realized trade result.

The one-second tape lacks full exchange quote age and complete ticks. Strict mode requires known freshness metadata; repeated unusable rows also prevent continuous 21-minute bar history. Zero strict trades demonstrate a recording/admission limitation, not a profitable safe strategy. Proxy losses describe these recorded observations and assumed aggressive fills. They do not quantify actual exchange fills, passive liquidity provision or true HFT behavior.

The OU rule's admission intersection produced no orders. We have not established a stable OU process or validated optimal stopping levels. Loosening its thresholds after seeing zero trades would be a new hypothesis and must use separate future evaluation.
