## Training coverage and transfer limits

There were 420 observed instrument/slot/day groups in the first three recordings. Strict metadata and gap checks admitted zero complete slots. Receipt-proxy checks admitted 237 complete slots, but only 21 instrument/slot combinations had all three training returns. The model refuses missing intervals and never imputes zero returns. [Strict training diagnostics](C:/Users/prajw/Downloads/Trader/research/14_intraday_seasonality/models/initial-v1/recent_trade.json) and [proxy estimates](C:/Users/prajw/Downloads/Trader/research/14_intraday_seasonality/models/initial-v1/receipt_proxy.json) retain coverage, per-day returns and input hashes.

The paper studies half-hour cross-sectional continuation using years of NYSE data. Our rules instead follow each stock's three-session historical slot mean or require three agreeing signs. We neither reproduce cross-sectional deciles nor test 40-trading-day persistence. The four evaluation dates were already observed in earlier research. All results remain historical diagnostics.

Quote midpoint returns remove a simple traded bid/ask alternation effect, but they do not prove fresh exchange quotes or executable quantities. Receipt-proxy mode tolerates unknown metadata explicitly. Source quote age remains unknown and the tape contains only one received observation per second. The common fill model crosses bid/ask with slippage and fees; passive fills, calibrated market impact and exchange latency remain untested.
