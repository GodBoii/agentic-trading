## What the state transitions showed

Each variant armed 415 setups across the seven proxy sessions. Reclaim observed nine completed-bar VWAP touches and emitted three signals. Only one filled after the common entry controls. Bounded pullback observed 24 touches, emitted nine signals and filled four. Reclaim expired 286 setups, while bounded pullback expired 282. The remaining outcomes include data resets, spread checks, invalidation and session-end setups; they should not be silently treated as executed trades.

The strategy's rare completed sequences explain its low turnover. Reducing expiry constraints or moving touch bands because the sample is small would change the hypothesis after seeing outcomes. Keep the frozen specification. More complete future recordings are needed before deciding whether the sequence occurs often enough and whether executable returns cover fees.

## Verification and limits

Six focused behavior tests pass. They cover separate arm/touch/confirmation bars, mirrored shorts, bounded trend-side pullback, missing VWAP, gaps and expiry without immediate same-bar rearming. Both proxy variants were repeated on August20 using their persisted configurations. All accounting fields and exact trade-file hashes match. [Repeatability evidence](C:/Users/prajw/Downloads/Trader/research/10_vwap_pullback/runs/initial-v1/repeatability-2026-08-20.json).

The source literature concerns VWAP execution benchmarks, not proof that VWAP support or reclaim predicts returns. Our explicit setup rules are new local hypotheses. Recorded VWAP weights, source quote age and true volume remain unverified, and midpoint bars sample only one received observation per second. The common execution model crosses bid/ask with fees and extra slippage but does not calibrate impact or true executable queue depth.

No live edge is established. All sessions had been observed by previous research and every variant remains ineligible for promotion. These results add a distinct continuation family to the registry with its complete negative and sparse evidence preserved.
