# Sources and what was actually used

Reviewed on October 9, 2026. External code was read, not executed. Source papers concern different targets and markets. This study is a local NSE adaptation, not a reproduction of their reported returns.

| Source | Review and method transferred | Difference in this experiment |
|---|---|---|
| [Gu, Kelly and Xiu, Empirical Asset Pricing via Machine Learning, published article](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/5/2223/5758276), also [SSRN 3306110](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3306110) | Read the original article's sample-splitting, penalized regression, regression-tree and random-forest sections. Trees average outcomes in partitions and forests average randomized bootstrap trees. The article separates training, calibration and testing chronologically. | Seven NSE survivors, nine daily technical inputs and next-session open-to-close returns. No CRSP monthly returns, fundamentals, macro interactions or reproduction of the paper's performance. Fixed parameters, no hyperparameter search. |
| [Rapach, Strauss and Zhou, SSRN 1257858](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1257858), [original PDF via Bocconi](https://didattica.unibocconi.it/mypage/dwload.php?nomefile=Rapach_Strauss_and_Zhou_RFS_200920200301133941.pdf) | Reviewed abstract and indexed original section 1.2 on weighted forecast combinations and ex ante weight estimation. Direct PDF fetch was unavailable through the browser. | Fixed inverse calibration MSE rather than recursive quarterly macro forecasts. No claim to have reviewed the entire PDF or reproduced the paper. |
| [gabbocg/rz2013, Table 6.2 source](https://github.com/gabbocg/rz2013/blob/master/table6_2.R) | Read actual source, especially lines 95-170. POOL-AVG takes an arithmetic average. POOL-DMSFE weights forecasts inversely by discounted past squared errors. The file also contains full-sample coefficient-sign calculations. | Independently implemented a static calibration-only inverse-MSE adaptation. No discounting or updating on evaluation outcomes. Did not copy the full-sample sign calculations, economic predictor data or R program. |
| [scikit-learn Ridge](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.linear_model.Ridge.html), [RandomForestRegressor](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.ensemble.RandomForestRegressor.html) | Official objective and implementation reference. Actual installed version is 1.8.0, recorded in the manifest, rather than assuming the latest online docs match the environment. | Use installed scikit-learn with parameters frozen in specification.json. No external repository installation. |
| [Rapach and Zhou, Forecasting Stock Returns, SSRN 4403635](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4403635) | Abstract-level context for instability, forecast combination and the historical-mean benchmark. | Context only. Full paper was not accessible, and no unreviewed formula is attributed to it. |

The SSRN landing page and several direct abstract/PDF links returned fetch errors. Indexed originals and the accessible published article supplied the stated evidence. Availability is documented rather than bypassed.

The fee function comes from the existing local research lab. Its archived September 30, 2026 tariff is a frozen counterfactual, not a newly verified current or historical tariff.
