## Causality and verification

Eight focused tests pass. They cover finite seeded fitting, variance floors, inadequate and degenerate training, posterior normalization, immutable past probabilities under later calls, completed-bar timing, gap resets, unknown strict metadata, invalid persisted model numbers and training-date rejection. The independent density model has no transition filter and no future-state smoother.

All four August 24 variant/mode replays were repeated from the persisted models without fitting again. Accounting summaries and exact trade-file SHA-256 hashes match. [Repeatability evidence](C:/Users/prajw/Downloads/Trader/research/17_regime_mixtures/runs/initial-v1/repeatability-2026-08-24.json).

Three development days cannot establish stable regime distributions. Feature windows overlap, stocks correlate, all later dates were previously observed, and source quote age remains unknown. Every result has promotion disabled. These losses support preserving the frozen model as a failed test, then obtaining fresh complete recordings before any new regime specification or evaluation period.
